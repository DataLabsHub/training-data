You are working on an ALE desktop benchmark task.

## Your Task
Use FlightGear 2024.1.1+ with the Cessna 172P to complete a three-phase GUI flight task at KSFO: pre-flight setup, live ILS approach, and self-replay instrument reading.

## Input Files
- `input/launch_args.txt`: required FlightGear launch arguments, including the logging configuration.
- `input/metar.txt`: METAR string to load for the session.
- `input/logging.xml`: 1 Hz property logger configuration.
- `input/procedure_brief.md`: local ILS Z RWY 28R briefing and chart facts used for Phase A values.

Aircraft, airport, and procedure:
- Aircraft: `c172p`
- Airport: `KSFO`
- Starting parking: `G_A4`
- Departure runway: `10R`
- Approach runway: `28R`
- Approach: `ILS Z RWY 28R`
- Alternate airport for map measurement: `KOAK`
- NAV1: `111.70` MHz
- NAV2: `115.80` MHz
- NAV1 OBS: `284` degrees magnetic
- Transponder squawk: `3662`
- Transponder mode: `ALT`
- Altimeter setting: `29.99` inHg

## Software
- Use FlightGear.
- Complete the task through the application's GUI or native workflow unless the visible task inputs explicitly allow a helper command for packaging or export.

## What You Must Do
1. Launch FlightGear through the Launcher GUI using `input/launch_args.txt` and the METAR from `input/metar.txt`.
2. Confirm the C172P starts at KSFO parking `G_A4`.
3. In the Map dialog, enable airports, navaids, and fixes. Measure KSFO ARP to KOAK ARP great-circle distance.
4. In Route Manager, build route `KSFO/10R -> SFO -> CEPIN -> KSFO/28R`; read total and per-leg distances and save `output/route.xml`.
5. In the cockpit GUI, set NAV1, NAV2, OBS, transponder, transponder mode, and altimeter to the values above.
6. In the property browser, inspect the requested aircraft, airport, navigation, and transponder properties and record them in `output/result.json`.
7. Capture screenshots `output/screenshots/A_radio_panel.png`, `A_map.png`, `A_route_manager.png`, and `A_property_browser.png`.

Phase B - live approach:
1. Reset or restart with the same launch arguments and METAR.
2. Take off from runway 10R at about 55 KIAS.
3. Climb on runway heading until passing 1500 ft AGL.
4. Turn right to heading 230 degrees magnetic and continue climbing downwind south of KSFO.
5. Level at 3000 ft MSL on heading 230 degrees.
6. Reconfirm NAV1 111.70 and engage KAP140 in order: AP ON, HDG, ALT, APR.
7. At approximately 7 NM south-southwest of KSFO ARP, turn right to heading 320 degrees magnetic to intercept the RWY 28R localizer from the south.
8. Allow localizer and glideslope capture, track the 3.00 degree glidepath, disconnect autopilot below 200 ft AGL before touchdown, and land on RWY 28R at about 60 KIAS with full flaps.
9. Save `output/live_approach.fgtape`, keep `output/live_property_log.csv`, and record `output/live_session.mp4`.

Phase C - self-replay analysis:
1. Reset or restart FlightGear and load your own `output/live_approach.fgtape` with the Flight Recorder GUI.
2. Scrub the replay to locate these six events: `T_takeoff`, `T_top_of_climb`, `T_loc_capture`, `T_gs_capture`, `T_ap_disconnect`, and `T_touchdown`.
3. At each event, read from the cockpit panel: magnetic heading, indicated altitude, indicated airspeed, vertical speed, and NAV1 CDI horizontal deviation.
4. Record the readings in `output/replay_readings.json` and capture `output/screenshots/C_T_<event>.png` for each event.
5. Record `output/replay_session.mp4`.
