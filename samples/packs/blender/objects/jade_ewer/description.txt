玉壶（asset_jadevase）
The Jade Ewer


【中文】

壶形取自唐宋执壶：鼓腹向上收成长颈，肩部一侧探出弯流，另一侧是同样弯转的把
手；盖作覆碗状，顶心立一颗金色宝珠钮。器身并不追求端正的对称——流的手腕向外
轻轻一挑，把手的弧线向内收拢，两条曲线在腹身两侧各让出一段空白，饱满的腹部
因此成为视线落下来的地方。

壶身的质感来自开片。青白釉面之下，细密的冰裂纹纵横交错；釉色清透，裂纹呈灰
绿，颈与肩处收得更密，越往腹部越疏。光线落上去，釉面泛起一层柔和的亮，裂纹
沉在亮光底下，像隔着一层薄冰看进去。顶上的金钮是全器唯一的异色，圆润明亮，
与通体青白之间留出一点轻松的落差。

作品以 Blender 建模完成，并附带参数化生成器：在真实尺寸（米）范围内调整器
身高度、腹径、足径、盖高、钮径与壁厚，一键重新生成。模型可导出为带贴图的
FBX 与 GLB，在 three.js 网页中实时呈现；观众能旋转、缩放、切换背景，从任意
角度端详这层釉与裂纹。

【English】

The form follows Tang and Song pouring vessels: a round belly drawn up
into a long neck, a curved spout stepping out from one shoulder and a
matching curved handle on the other; the lid is shaped like an inverted
bowl, with a small gold bead finial standing at its center. The body does
not aim for formal symmetry. The spout lifts its wrist lightly outward
while the handle draws its arc inward, and the two curves leave a stretch
of open space on either side, so the full belly becomes the place where
the eye comes to rest.

Its character comes from the crackle glaze. Beneath the pale celadon
surface a fine web of ice-crackle runs in every direction; the glaze is
clear while the cracks read grey-green, denser at the neck and shoulder
and opening wider across the belly. Where light lands, the glaze lifts
into a soft sheen and the cracks sink beneath it, as if seen through a
thin sheet of ice. The gold finial on top is the only other color in the
piece: round and bright, a small and easy step away from the
celadon around it.

The piece is modeled in Blender and ships with a parametric generator that
rebuilds it at real-world scale, in meters, from body height, belly and
foot diameter, lid height, finial diameter and wall thickness. The model
exports to textured FBX and GLB and runs in a real-time three.js viewer,
where visitors can orbit, zoom and switch backdrops, studying the glaze
and its cracks from any angle.

UV 使用说明（v004 明确保留的共享方式）
壶身基础网格分成沿 X 轴两侧的两片连续 UV 岛，覆盖颈、腹、足及流、把的两侧；单片内部没有面积重叠，两片之间部分区域共享同一瓷纹。壶身 UV 有一部分超出 0–1，使用 Repeat 重复采样；这些瓷纹按可重复表面纹理使用，不是每个表面位置唯一的图案。
Solidify 加厚生成的内壁沿用对应外壁的 UV，壶身、瓷盖及金钮均保留这种对应采样。瓷盖与壶身使用同一组瓷纹；基础瓷盖与金钮各有独立 UV 岛，金钮使用独立金属材质与贴图。不同材质的坐标数值相同不代表混用纹理。
本版有意保留以上共享和重复，以保持已经交付的纹理外观；没有把它描述成无重叠的独占图集。适用于当前统一瓷釉和金属表面；如需只在单侧添加文字、独立污渍或逐面唯一烘焙，应先另做独占 UV 再烘焙。

UV reuse retained in v004
The base body has two continuous UV islands on opposite X sides, covering the two sides of the neck, belly, foot, spout and handle. Each island has no internal area overlap; the two islands partially reuse the same porcelain pattern. Some body UVs extend outside 0–1 and use Repeat sampling. The porcelain maps are used as repeatable surface textures, not as a unique pattern for every surface location.
The inner surfaces produced by Solidify inherit the corresponding outer-surface UVs; this correspondence is retained on the body, porcelain lid and gold finial. The porcelain lid and body use the same porcelain maps. The base lid and finial have separate UV islands, and the finial uses its own metal material/maps; equal UV coordinates across different materials do not mean shared texture content.
This version deliberately retains those overlaps and repeats to preserve the delivered appearance. This is not a non-overlapping unique atlas. It suits the current uniform glaze and metal treatment. Before adding one-sided lettering, unique stains or a per-face bake, create a dedicated non-overlapping UV set and rebake.
